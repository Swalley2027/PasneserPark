FAQ:

What is included in a stay?
Bed linens, towels, use of shared kitchen and bathroom, WiFi, and access to all facilities.

Are pets welcome?
Yes, pets are welcome at PasneserPark.

How do I get to PasneserPark?
PasneserPark is located in Shelcan, near Elbasan. We offer airport transfers. Coordinates: 41.071076, 20.140247

Is there electricity and WiFi?
Yes, each tent has electricity and there is free WiFi on the premises.

Can I make a campfire?
Campfires are allowed outside of peak season. In summer, this is not permitted due to fire risk.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash payment in Euro or Albanian Lek.

What activities are there in the area?
Hiking, cycling (bikes for rent), visiting local villages, wildlife spotting, stargazing, and enjoying peace and quiet.
